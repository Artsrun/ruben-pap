A note from the studio

Made slowly.
Kept close.

Ruben Pap is an independent ceramic practice rooted in the rhythm of making. Working with local clay and a pared-back palette, Ruben creates considered objects that welcome use, age, and touch.

No two pieces are exactly alike. Every curve and surface carries a little evidence of the hand that made it.
