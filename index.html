    About the artist
    
Ruben Pap

    
Ruben began learning ceramics at 22, working in a Yerevan studio and spending a decade experimenting with form, clay and glaze.

    
In 2012 he opened his own studio. His practice moves freely between art, design and functional ware — combining traditional wheel-throwing with cutting and altering that gives each piece a linear, living structure.

    
Layered glazes over warm brown clay are his signature, from deep turquoise to the bronze surfaces of his newest work.

    
“When I am making a piece, I never know what is going to happen.”— Ruben Pap

Process
From raw clay to the final firing, every step happens by hand in the studio.

  

    

      
      01
Throwing
Forms start on the wheel, centred and pulled from a single piece of clay.

    

    

      
      02
Altering
Rims are folded, cut and pierced while the clay is still soft.

    

    

      
      03
Glazing
Glazes are mixed in the studio and layered over natural clay.

    

    

      
      04
Finishing
Each piece is cleaned, checked and finished by hand — never exactly the same twice.

    

  






  
The studio
Komitas Street, Yerevan. Shelves of finished work, a wall of glaze, and a long table where pieces are shaped and shown.
